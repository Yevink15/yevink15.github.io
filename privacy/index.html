Privacy Notice

Last updated November 16, 2026.

Information you choose to send

The contact form asks for your name, email address, event date, coverage type, and message. Phone is optional. Form submissions are sent to FormSubmit for email delivery to YKCaptures. FormSubmit says it retains submissions for 30 days; the delivered email may remain in the recipient’s mailbox.

If you request original photos or send a photo selection, the information you provide for that request, such as your name, contact details, selected photo identifiers, and note, is sent to the YKCaptures HomeServer to process the request.

Please do not send passwords, payment details, government identifiers, or other sensitive information through these forms.

Analytics and external services

This site uses Google Analytics to measure site usage. It also loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser connects to those services when the relevant page resources load. You can manage cookies and site data through your browser settings.

The public site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Contact form submissions are handled by FormSubmit, and photo request submissions are handled by the YKCaptures HomeServer.

Choices and requests

You can contact YKCaptures directly to ask about a submission or request its deletion. Email YevinKariyawasam@gmail.com. You may also use the social links on the contact page.

This notice may be updated as the site or its services change.
